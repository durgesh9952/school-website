ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A Place Where Education Meets Excellence

Shri Shivajirao Chaudhari English Medium School & Junior College, Nardana, is committed to providing quality education in a positive and supportive learning environment.

Our school focuses on academic excellence, discipline, character building, creativity and the overall development of every student.

We believe that every student has unique abilities and deserves proper guidance, encouragement and opportunities to achieve their goals.
